# Telecom Customer Retention Best Practices

This document outlines proven, actionable strategies for retaining telecommunications customers who show signs of churning.

## High Monthly Charges
1. **Targeted Discounting**
   Offer a 3-month 15% loyalty discount for customers whose monthly bill consistently exceeds the territory average by 20% or more. This temporary relief often halts immediate cancellation while resetting value perception.

2. **Plan Right-Sizing**
   Proactively audit data usage and switch customers who are overpaying for unused data/services to a customized, lower-tier plan. Communicating this savings builds immense trust.

3. **Bundled Value-Add**
   Provide complimentary access to a premium streaming service (e.g., Netflix, Spotify) for 6 to 12 months instead of dropping the base plan price. This increases sticky utility without degrading your core pricing model.

## Short Tenure
4. **Onboarding Concierge**
   Assign a dedicated onboarding specialist during the critical first 90 days of service. This specialist ensures a smooth hardware setup, bill explanation, and validates early network performance.

5. **Early Upgrade Path**
   Allow high-value new customers to upgrade their primary device with activation fees waived after just 6 months of continuous, on-time payments, locking them into a fresh hardware cycle early.

6. **Milestone Rewards Data**
   Grant a block of free international calling minutes or a permanent 5GB data bump at the 3-month and 6-month marks to reward early loyalty and reinforce the habit of retention.

## Poor Customer Service Experience
7. **Dedicated Support Routing**
   Assign a dedicated Level 2 tech support agent (or prioritize queue placement) to customers who have contacted standard support more than twice within a single 30-day period.

8. **Proactive Management Follow-up**
   Mandate a courtesy check-in call from a customer success manager 48 hours after a negative (CSAT < 3) survey response to de-escalate frustration and immediately course-correct.

## Multiple Service Outages
9. **Automatic Pro-rated Credits**
   Automatically calculate and apply bill credits corresponding to the duration of any localized outage without requiring the customer to call in or lobby for compensation.

10. **Hardware Refresh Program**
    Offer a complimentary, expedited router/modem upgrade to users experiencing repeated, documented connectivity drops that are linked to deprecated or failing legacy home equipment.
